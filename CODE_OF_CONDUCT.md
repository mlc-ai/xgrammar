# Code of Conduct

XGrammar adopts the behavior standards and definitions in the [LF Projects Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/). We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and professional community in which everyone can participate without harassment or discrimination. All participants share responsibility for maintaining this environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all participants in XGrammar's repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, community channels, and project meetings and events. It also applies when representing XGrammar in other public spaces.

## Community standards

The full standards and definitions are available in the [LF Projects Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/). In particular, participants are expected to:

- Treat others fairly and respectfully, including during disagreements or urgent work.
- Keep the community free of harassment, bullying, sexual harassment, and discrimination based on personal characteristics or group membership.
- Discuss ideas constructively, accept and offer useful criticism, and avoid insults, personal attacks, and demeaning language.
- Recognize others' contributions and be honest about qualifications and conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy and confidential information. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.
- Respect cultural differences and personal boundaries, and help address inappropriate behavior when it occurs.
- Keep discussions relevant to the channel and considerate of their audience.
- Hand over responsibilities considerately when stepping away from project work.

## Reporting

For XGrammar incidents, contact the project privately at [ubospica@gmail.com](mailto:ubospica@gmail.com). Include a description of what happened and any relevant dates, links, or supporting information. Please do not post sensitive reports in public issues or discussions.

If a report concerns the person receiving that email, contact another uninvolved maintainer privately using the public contact information on their GitHub profile. The maintainer profiles are linked in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Enforcement and appeals

Uninvolved maintainers review reports and determine an appropriate response. A maintainer who is the subject of a report or has another conflict of interest must not investigate or decide that report. Reports are handled as privately as possible, and information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a request to stop the behavior, a warning, removal of inappropriate content, or temporary or permanent restrictions on participation. Maintainers seek a fair resolution and communicate the outcome to the people involved while respecting privacy.

Appeals can be sent through the reporting channels above. They are reviewed by uninvolved maintainers who did not make the original decision.
